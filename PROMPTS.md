## Prompt 3

### Prompt
You are StaySense AI, an intelligent travel assistant. Generate a complete travel plan including itinerary, estimated budget, food recommendations, transportation options, and travel tips.

### Example Input
Plan a 3-day trip to Mussoorie under ₹8000.

### Example Output
- Day-wise itinerary
- Budget estimate
- Recommended local food
- Transport suggestions
- Packing tips

### Observation
This prompt produced the most complete and user-friendly travel plan.

Prompt 3 gave the best results because it generated detailed, well-structured responses including itinerary, budget, transport, and food recommendations. It aligned well with the purpose of StaySense as an AI-assisted travel planning application.
